# Hi, I'm Randall

English · [中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

I'm a product designer exploring ideas through working prototypes, small products, and reusable tools. I use AI coding agents to make ideas tangible, examine the decisions inside them, and find what needs another iteration.

## Questions behind the work

- **Where is the real friction?** I want to understand the person's goal, the surrounding workflow, and which part is worth changing.
- **How much should the product decide?** I'm exploring the balance between a useful default, automation, and a person's ability to shape the result.
- **What makes the whole experience hold together?** I'm thinking through first use, reasons to return, failure states, and the cost of keeping something useful.

These questions guide my experiments. The projects below explore different parts of them.

## Selected work

### [Web to Hi-Fi Figma](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/web-to-figma-hi-fi)
**Keeping design editable after generation**

A workflow for turning implemented web pages into editable Figma layouts, with text, Auto Layout, components, and visual checks. It grew from wanting to carry a working web idea back into the design process.

### [Bloom Studio](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/bloom-studio)
**Giving people a hand in generated results**

A flower design studio that combines image analysis, procedural geometry, and controls for shape, colour, and blooming motion. I'm exploring how a generated starting point and direct manipulation can work together.

### [UI/UX News Digest](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/uiux-news-digest)
**Making scattered updates useful for design work**

A workflow that checks original sources, filters a week's product and interface updates, and builds a visual briefing. The design problem is deciding what deserves attention and explaining why it matters.

### [Move Club](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/move-club-motion)
**Learning how interaction feels by making it**

An ongoing collection of playable interaction and motion studies. I rebuild small moments to examine timing, continuity, feedback, and spatial relationships.

[Explore the live collection](https://move-club-motion.vercel.app)

## More experiments

- [GitHub Skill Radar](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/github-skill-radar) — a configurable workflow for finding emerging agent skills and narrowing them to a review set.
- [Hatch My Codex Pet](https://github.com/RandallisLeo/hatch-my-codex-pet) — a guided character-design workflow built around visible choices and approvals.

This GitHub is a growing record of what I make, the decisions behind it, and how my understanding of design and products changes along the way.
